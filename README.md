# Human–AI Collaborative Translation Ethics: Research Materials

This repository contains the coding records, analytical matrices, revision logs, frequency tables, and historical research snapshots supporting the article **“A Unified Ethical Framework for Human–AI Collaborative Translation.”**

## Repository structure

- `current/` — the current coding framework, corpus classification, theme-synthesis matrices, evidence chain, supplementary tables, and revision logs.
- `archives/` — dated snapshots of researcher-generated coding and analysis files from earlier stages of the project.
- `SOURCE_MANIFEST.md` — the 29-document corpus and its analytical classification.
- `ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.csv` — provenance, checksums, and retained/omitted file counts for each historical archive.

## Corpus and method

The study analyses 29 normative documents: 17 professional ethics documents and 12 documents on translation technology ethics or governance. Text coding produced 29 descriptive themes, which were synthesised into six analytical themes and subsequently used, together with normative ethical reasoning, to justify four principles.

## Copyright and reproducibility note

The repository does **not** redistribute third-party standards, professional codes, position papers, or other source documents. Those texts remain with their respective rights holders and are identified in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.md`. The archived ZIP files have therefore been reconstructed to retain only researcher-generated coding, statistics, matrices, notes, and visualisations; raw source documents and nested archives that may contain them were omitted. Readers should consult the issuing organisations for authoritative copies of the source texts.

Some historical ZIP files existed in multiple identical copies. Exact duplicates are represented once. The archive manifest records the preserved public package and its SHA-256 checksum.
